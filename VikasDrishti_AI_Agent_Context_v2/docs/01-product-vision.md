# VIKASDRISHTI — PRODUCT VISION

## One-line definition

**VikasDrishti is an AI-powered infrastructure project intelligence layer that turns project monitoring data into forward-looking risk, explainability, early warnings and actionable interventions.**

## Product transformation

Traditional monitoring:

Data
→ Status
→ Report

VikasDrishti:

Data
→ Predict
→ Explain
→ Warn
→ Prescribe
→ Act
→ Verify
→ Learn

## Four-role intelligence ecosystem

### Minister / Policymaker
**Portfolio Intelligence → Prioritize Intervention**

The policymaker needs a national/portfolio view:
- where risk is concentrated;
- which projects may require intervention;
- where cost exposure is increasing;
- where schedule risk is high;
- how projects compare with peers;
- which interventions should receive priority.

### Ministry / Project Management
**Project Intelligence → Assign & Escalate**

The project manager needs:
- detailed project intelligence;
- model predictions;
- explanations;
- early warnings;
- recommendations;
- intervention assignment;
- escalation;
- progress and financial monitoring.

### Field Officer
**Ground Intelligence → Verify & Track**

The field officer needs:
- assigned projects;
- ground-level status;
- progress updates;
- issues;
- evidence;
- verification;
- corrective-action tracking.

### Contractor / Implementer
**Execution Intelligence → Resolve & Update**

The contractor needs:
- assigned projects;
- assigned tasks;
- milestones;
- monthly targets;
- target achievement;
- execution issues;
- progress/evidence updates.

## Product pillars

### 1. Predictive intelligence
Predict:
- future cost-overrun category;
- future delay category;
- future project risk.

### 2. Explainable intelligence
Use SHAP to answer:
**Why did the model produce this prediction?**

### 3. Early warning
Turn important predictions and indicators into actionable warnings.

### 4. Prescriptive intelligence
Answer:
**What should the responsible person do next?**

### 5. Benchmarking
Compare a project against:
- sector;
- ministry;
- state where meaningful;
- similar projects;
- agency history where data supports it.

### 6. Integrated workflow
Connect policymakers, managers, field officers and contractors.

### 7. Enrichment feedback loop
Daily field/contractor inputs become a future enriched dataset rather than being silently inserted into the current model.

## Product language

- Project Intelligence
- Portfolio Intelligence
- Ground Intelligence
- Execution Intelligence
- Future Risk
- Next-Snapshot Forecast
- Risk Drivers
- Early Warning

- sales pipeline
- tickets
- customer success
- generic project CRM terminology.

## Source framing

The SIH PPT describes the intended platform as an integrated intelligence ecosystem and explicitly frames the transformation as monitoring → predictive → prescriptive. The PPT also proposes SHAP, RAG assistant, CUF-vs-enriched analysis, geospatial risk mapping and intervention workflows.

Treat external integrations shown in the PPT as planned unless implemented in the prototype.
